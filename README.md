# 癲佬被拘（《東華報》1913-02-22 第7版，澳洲國家圖書館） / Dian lao bei ju [Police Detention Following a Reported Mental Health Crisis]: Transcription Dataset

《東華報》1913-02-22 刊載的〈癲佬被拘〉。《本洲新聞》報導一名從鄉間來雪梨的華人工人，記報中稱其突然發癲狂、他人受傷送醫，以及警察拘押候訊。保留《東華報》當時對被拘者的描述。

Dian lao bei ju [Police Detention Following a Reported Mental Health Crisis] appeared in Tung Wah Times (1913-02-22). Sydney local news reports a Chinese worker described by the newspaper as experiencing mental distress, the medical treatment of injured persons, and his detention by the police pending a hearing.

## V0.2.0

完整收錄第7版此篇原題與正文 12 個原欄，全文格式為 TXT、JSON 與 TEI XML。比對獨立原圖讀法後回圖裁定，採用 4 筆訂正，留存疑讀定位。

TXT, JSON and TEI XML cover the complete separately headed article, comprising the heading and 12 source columns on page 7. An independent image reading was compared with the first transcription. Source-image adjudication adopted 4 changes and retained unresolved readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。照錄原題、時代用字與原欄次。疑讀按欄記在 JSON。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Headings, historical terminology and source column order are preserved. Uncertainties are recorded by column. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
